# Library And Papers

## Role Of `Library/Papers/`

`Library/Papers/` is the canonical storage location for PDF papers in this vault.

- It holds source documents, not their full semantic interpretation.
- Papers are first-class objects, not generic attachments.
- The folder should remain easy to scan and safe to manage from a cold start.

## Papers As First-Class Objects

Papers deserve explicit treatment because they often support multiple notes, claims, and future synthesis work.

- Keep the PDF as a stable source object.
- Do not bury papers inside topic folders by default.
- Do not treat papers as incidental assets attached to a single note.

## Relationship To Notes

Each meaningful paper should have a corresponding note in `Notes/` when useful.

- the PDF in `Library/Papers/` is the canonical source file
- the paper note in `Notes/` is the semantic interface for summaries, links, quotes, claims, and follow-on thinking

The note and PDF should stay clearly associated through names, links, and local metadata where helpful.

## Cold-Start Policy

The paper library must be easy to use before a large corpus exists.

- Start with a flat paper corpus.
- Prefer consistent filenames over early hierarchy.
- Avoid domain- or project-specific paper folders until the volume and usage patterns clearly justify them.

## Default Storage Policy

At the beginning, `Library/Papers/` should remain flat.

- No default topic-based hierarchy
- No automatic subfolders by author, year, venue, or field
- No speculative taxonomies

If more structure is needed later, it should be introduced deliberately and incrementally.
